What to budget for

The lease-extension premium is separate from your solicitor, valuation surveyor and the freeholder's reasonable legal and valuation costs under the current statutory route. For early scenario planning only, the calculator starts with editable professional-fee allowances; these are not local quotations.

Never exchange contracts on a short-lease property until a qualified solicitor has checked the lease, lender suitability, title, management information and extension strategy. Auction bids can create immediate legal obligations.
